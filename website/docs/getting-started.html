Getting started#

Bobba Hotel is a Habbo-style hotel you run yourself. It's a Garry's Mod gamemode: rooms you build and decorate, avatars, chat, a shop, trading, friends, groups, wired - the hotel you remember, but yours. Play alone, with friends, or run a public hotel on a server. Everything in it - furni, clothes, rooms, windows, wired boxes, phone apps - can be added or changed by addons from the Steam Workshop.

There's also a desktop app that runs the same hotel without Garry's Mod, and players on the desktop app and in Garry's Mod can be in the same hotel.

In development. Bobba Hotel isn't on the Workshop yet. What's here works and is played and tested every day, but things still change quickly. See the changelog for what's new and the roadmap for what's coming.

What you need#

  • Garry's Mod (any recent version). Nothing else to install: GMod has the database built in.
  • For Habbo's furni, clothes and look: the Habbo content addon (bobba_habbo), which downloads them from Habbo while you play. See The Habbo addon.
  • Or the desktop app instead of Garry's Mod (Windows; see The desktop app).

Installing (for now)#

Until it's on the Workshop, Bobba Hotel comes from its GitHub repository:

  1. Download the repository (the green Code button, Download ZIP) and unpack it.
  2. Copy into your garrysmod folder (usually Steam/steamapps/common/GarrysMod/garrysmod): - gamemodes/bobbahotel into garrysmod/gamemodes/ - addons/bobbahotel (the hotel's fonts) and addons/bobba_habbo (Habbo's content) into garrysmod/addons/
  3. Start Garry's Mod and pick Bobba Hotel in the gamemode list (bottom right of the main menu).

Starting a hotel#

How What happens
Singleplayer Start a game on any map. You see the start screen, check in to your hotel, and play alone.
Peer to peer / listen server Start with more than one player slot; friends join you from the server browser or Steam. You're the hotel's owner.
Dedicated server srcds -game garrysmod +gamemode bobbahotel +map gm_construct. Give yourself superadmin (or use ULX / SAM) to manage the hotel.
Desktop app Open Bobba Hotel.exe, add a hotel or host one. See The desktop app.

The map doesn't matter: the hotel draws its own rooms over it.

Your first minutes#

  1. The start screen (when you host): your hotels and the ones you've added. Check in.
  2. The hotel's page: news, who's online, your friends. Click Enter hotel.
  3. Pick a starter room: new players choose a decorated room of their own, with a gift inside.
  4. A short tutorial: walk, open the gift, move a furni, say something - and get a few credits.

From there, Playing explains the hotel, and Running a hotel the Manage screen, settings and permissions.

Two looks#

Bobba Hotel has two complete user interfaces, and the hotel picks one (players can pick for themselves):

  • Classic - Habbo's own windows, as nitro-react has them: the toolbar, navigator, catalogue, inventory, friend list, messenger, wired windows. The Habbo pack starts in this one.
  • Phone and tablet - Bobba Hotel's own look: a phone you take out of your pocket and a tablet for the bigger apps, with a home screen, widgets, notifications and an app for everything.

Switch with bobba_ui habbo or bobba_ui phone in the console, or in Settings.
