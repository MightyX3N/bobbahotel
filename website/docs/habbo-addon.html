The Habbo addon#

bobba_habbo is the content pack that makes Bobba Hotel look and feel like Habbo: Habbo's furni, clothes, rooms, chat bubbles, sounds, icons and windows. Nothing of Habbo's is in the addon. Each player's game downloads what it needs from Habbo's own servers, the way Habbo's own client does, and keeps it in a cache.

It lives apart from the gamemode on purpose: a hotel can switch it off and use other content instead, and the gamemode never depends on it.

What it does, step by step#

  1. Finds Habbo's files. It reads Habbo's public client settings (external_variables), which say where the current furni, figure and text files are.
  2. Reads Habbo's data: furnidata (every furni: name, size, whether you can sit, stand or lie on it), figuredata (every piece of clothing and colour), and Habbo's texts (so windows say what Habbo says).
  3. Downloads furni and clothes when they're first needed - a furni the first time someone sees it, not all 13 000 at once. Files are Habbo's Flash files (SWF); the addon unpacks them in Lua into pictures and their XML.
  4. Draws them the way Nitro does. The gamemode has Nitro's furniture visualizations and avatar renderer rewritten in Lua (layers, directions, animations, colours, shadows), and the addon feeds them Habbo's pictures and XML.
  5. Caches everything in garrysmod/data/bobbahotel/habbo/ so the next start is instant.

Unpacking happens in small jobs that give the frame back, so loading never stutters the room; pictures are written uncompressed (ten times quicker); six downloads run at once; and things you're about to need are fetched ahead.

What it adds to the hotel#

Part From Habbo
Furni Every floor and wall furni, with Habbo's behaviour (dice, gates, teleports, rollers, trophies, stickies, mood lights, TVs, wired boxes...)
The catalogue Built from furnidata: every furni by its line, families over the years under one page (Habboween 2009 - 2026, Christmas, ...), seasonal pages only in season, Habbo Club furni on the club's tab, rares on a staff tab, posters, stickies, and the Spaces tab (wallpapers, floors, landscapes)
Avatars Habbo's clothes, colours, effects and actions, checked against figuredata like Habbo's server does
Rooms Habbo's room layouts, wallpapers, floors and landscapes; six classic starter rooms
The look Nitro's windows (the classic UI) with Habbo's icons from Habbo's desktop client, the chat bubbles, sounds, the hotel view and a Habbo-style hotel page

Settings#

  • Club furni for members only (on): Habbo Club's furni can only be bought by members, as in Habbo.

Shop prices, seasons and clubs are the hotel's own settings (see Running a hotel).

If Habbo can't be reached#

A furni that can't be downloaded isn't drawn (and the server leaves furni it can't identify out of the room until it can); avatars fall back to a stand-in figure. Everything already in the cache keeps working offline.

Making your own pack#

The Habbo addon is the biggest example of what a content pack does: it answers the hotel's BOBBA.items.resolver, provides BOBBA.room.furniDrawer / furniInfo / furniIcon, the catalogue provider, starter rooms, plane styles and a hotel page. A pack with its own art does the same with its own pictures. See Making an addon.
