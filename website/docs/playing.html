Playing#

Everything here works in both looks - the classic Habbo windows and the phone. Where they differ, the phone's way is in brackets.

Rooms#

  • The navigator (the rooms button, or the phone's Rooms app) lists the hotel's own rooms, everyone's rooms (the busiest first), rooms with an event on, and your own and favourite rooms. Search by name, owner:, tag: or anything.
  • Making a room: pick a layout (Habbo's, from the small starter room to huge halls; some are for Habbo Club), a name and who may come in. You can own up to 25.
  • Room settings (the room info, the cog): name, description, category, tags, who can come in (open, doorbell, password, invisible), how many, who can trade, pets, walking through others, wall and floor thickness, chat settings (bubble width, speed, hearing distance, flood protection), who may kick, ban and mute, the rights list and the ban list.
  • Rights: give someone rights from the avatar menu and they can move and place furni and use wired.
  • Your home room: the house icon in the room info. You come back there after checking in.
  • The floor plan editor: draw your own room shape and heights, the door and wall height (Habbo Club, unless the hotel opens it to everyone).

Moving around#

  • Click a tile to walk there, Habbo's way.
  • Free movement: the arrow keys (or WASD, or a controller's stick) move your avatar freely; let go and you settle on the tile. Choose the keys in Settings: arrow keys, WASD while the chat is empty, or WASD with Enter to chat. A room can allow clicking only, free movement only, or both.
  • Typing never moves you: click into any text box (or the chat) and your keys are only letters until you click the room again.
  • The interact button (Space, or E with WASD; A on a controller) uses whatever is next to you; Tab picks the next thing when there's more than one.
  • Zoom: Ctrl + scroll (or scroll alone, bobba_zoom_with_ctrl 0).

Chat#

Talk, shout (Shift + Enter, or :shout text) and whisper (:whisper name text, or Whisper in the avatar menu). Bubbles look like Habbo's, with your chosen bubble style. The chat history is in the room tools. Rooms can set how far you're heard and how fast bubbles move.

Your avatar#

  • Clothes: the avatar editor (click yourself, My clothes; the phone's Clothes app). Club clothes and colours for members. Ten saved looks in the wardrobe.
  • Actions: dance (four more with Habbo Club), wave, blow a kiss, laugh, sit on the floor, signs. Idle for two minutes and you fall asleep.
  • Effects: hoverboards, spotlights, sparkles and more, worn from the editor's Effects tab.
  • Your profile: click your name. Your motto (shown in the room), when you joined, your friends, groups and relationships.

Furni#

  • The shop (catalogue; the phone's Furni app): every floor and wall furni of the content pack, by line, with a search. Seasonal furni only in their season. Buy several at once, or gift one to someone, with a message and your face on the tag.
  • The inventory (the phone's Bag): your furni, newest first. Place in room, then click where; R turns it; place several in a row.
  • In the room: click a furni for its info - Use, Rotate (R), Move (drag), Pick up.
  • What furni do: seats and beds, dice, bottles and wheels that roll, gates, one-way gates, rollers that carry people and furni, trophies with an engraving, stickies, the mood light, the background toner, the stacking helper, TVs that play videos, and teleports.
  • Teleports come unlinked. Click one of yours, Link, and pick which of your teleports it takes you to - in any of your rooms. Several can lead to the same one. One with nowhere to go lets you back out.

Money and Habbo Club#

Credits, duckets and diamonds. Everyone starts with some and is paid every so often while online; staff can give more. Furni costs credits; Habbo Club (or VIP) is bought with diamonds and gives club clothes, dances, room layouts and club furni. Nothing in the hotel is bought with real money.

Trading#

Click someone, Trade. Both of you put furni in (up to nine kinds), both accept, a short countdown, both confirm - and the furni change hands. Any change takes back both accepts, and the server checks every furni again at the end. Rooms decide who may trade (no one, people with rights, everyone).

Friends, messages, groups#

  • Friends: the friend list (the phone's Messages), requests, search by name, relationships (heart, smile, bobba). The friend bar shows who's online, with chat, follow and profile.
  • The messenger: chats with friends and your groups, room invites.
  • Groups: make one (a name, colour, a room of its own), members and admins, text channels. Join open groups, ask to join closed ones.
  • Events: put on an event in your room; it shows in the navigator's events.

Camera and videos#

  • The camera (toolbar; the phone's Camera): a roll of five shots of the room. Buy one as a photo to hang on a wall, or make it your room's picture in the navigator.
  • Videos: rooms have a TV queue (the tablet's Videos app): paste a YouTube link, everyone watches in sync on the room's TV furni; half the room can vote to skip.

The phone and tablet#

In the phone look, F2 (or the button in the corner) takes the phone out. It has a lock screen, a home screen you arrange (apps, widgets, pages, the dock), the shade with quick settings and notifications, and an App Store for addons' apps. Bigger apps open on the tablet.
