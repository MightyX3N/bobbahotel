What's new

Every development build so far, newest first. Bobba Hotel isn't released yet - these are the steps on the way.

0.9Wired

4 November 2026
  • Wired: Habbo's triggers, effects, conditions and add-ons, run the way Habbo's server does: stacks on a tile, conditions that all have to hold, effects with delays, random and unseen add-ons. Nitro's wired windows, choosing furni by clicking them in the room (marked in blue). See Wired and scripts.
  • Script boxes: Habbo's Custom Wired effect and condition run a short Lua script - move and switch furni, whisper, teleport, count and remember, wait. Sandboxed (no money, files, network or server) and held to step, memory and per-room limits, so an endless loop is stopped, not the hotel. Mistakes show in the box.
  • Teleports link one way: come unlinked, a Link window picks which of your teleports one takes you to (any room, several to one); trading one away unlinks it.
  • Typing never moves you: clicking into any text box (or the chat) turns free movement off until you click the room; R and the phone key are letters while you type.

0.8Running a hotel

4 November 2026
  • The Manage screen for the hotel's owner and admins: its look (name, about, news, the UI players start in, its colour, its page), its addons (on or off, restart) and their settings, the hotel's own settings, and the money log.
  • The start screen in Garry's Mod when you host: pick a hotel, add servers.
  • Trading, Habbo's way: offer, accept, the countdown, confirm - checked again in one transaction at the end. In the classic inventory and the phone's Bag.
  • The economy's log: every credit, club day and furni in or out, why and with whom (bobba_economy). Buying pays and gives together or not at all.
  • The classic look's social windows: the friend list with relationships and search, room invites, the messenger with groups' chats, the friend bar, profiles with mottos, and group windows (info, members, making one, managing it).

0.7The phone

4 November 2026
  • The phone and tablet, Bobba Hotel's own look: a lock screen, a home screen you arrange (apps, widgets, pages, the dock), the shade with quick tiles, notifications.
  • Apps for everything: Rooms, Bag, Wallet, Camera, Settings, the Furni, Clothes and Club shops, the floor plan, Messages, Groups (with text channels), Calls, Events, Videos and the App Store for addons' apps.
  • Checking in: a new start screen, the hotel's own page, and addons' Lua sandboxed in the desktop app.
  • Videos: a TV queue per room, played in sync on the room's TV furni, skip votes.
  • Camera photos with furni and people in Garry's Mod; text that would run off the screen wraps.

0.6Moving freely

4 November 2026
  • Steering: walk with the arrow keys, WASD or a controller; an interact button; keys and buttons you can change.
  • Free movement: move at any angle and settle on the tile when you stop; smooth on stairs; others see you glide. Rooms choose clicking, free movement or both. Walking starts at once.
  • The floor plan editor: tiles, heights, the door, wall height and thickness; import and export.
  • Avatar actions: dances, wave, kiss, laugh, signs, sitting on the floor, idle and blinking, effects, the wardrobe.
  • The camera: a roll of five, photos for your walls, room thumbnails.
  • A short tutorial for new players; bobba_reset_player.
  • Rooms' chat settings work; the navigator's room info popover.

0.5Rooms

4 November 2026
  • Room settings (all five tabs), rights, the doorbell and passwords, kick / ban / mute, deleting rooms, favourites and the home room, categories and tags, the avatar menu and infostand, the hotel view.
  • Starter rooms: new players pick a decorated room of their own (packs add them), with a gift inside.
  • Windows follow the game window's size.
  • The roadmap.

0.4The desktop app

3 November 2026
  • The desktop app (test build): the hotel without Garry's Mod, its server on a thread, a start page like the old Habbo website, its own icon, borderless full screen.
  • A leave button in the me menu.

0.3Habbo's windows and the shop

3 November 2026
  • Habbo's UI from nitro-react: the toolbar, the me menu, the purse, the room tools, the navigator with real rooms, the inventory. Sized for your screen (bobba_ui_scale).
  • The catalogue, inventory, credits, duckets, diamonds and Habbo Club (HC, VIP or both); every floor furni, seasonal furni in season, gifts, the staff rares tab.
  • Furni that do things: dice, bottles and wheels, gates, one-way gates, rollers, teleports, trophies, the background toner; building tools (stacking helper, placing in a row).
  • Wall furni: posters, windows, shelves, wallpapers, floors, landscapes, stickies, the mood light.
  • Habbo's sounds; R turns the furni being placed.

0.2Habbo's content

3 November 2026
  • The Habbo addon: downloads and unpacks Habbo's files on each player's PC; nothing of Habbo's shipped.
  • Avatars, furniture (Nitro's visualizations in Lua: placing, sitting, lying), floors and wallpapers.
  • Chat with Habbo's bubbles, Nitro's layout and word breaks.
  • The avatar editor with Habbo's clothes and icons; saving looks.
  • No stutter while loading: work in jobs, quicker pictures, downloads fetched ahead; Nitro's font.

0.1A new start

3 November 2026
  • Bobba Hotel starts over as a Garry's Mod gamemode: the core, addons, permissions (CAMI / ULX / SAM), saving, languages.
  • Rooms drawn the way Nitro draws them - geometry, camera and picking in Lua - with Habbo's colours, the door hole and walls lined up with Habbo's.
  • Rooms on the server: entering, walking with Habbo's pathfinder.
  • Zoom with Ctrl + scroll, smooth, towards the mouse.
